FAQ

Questions, answered

When can I download Mayotti?

Mayotti is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. This page will link straight to it as soon as it's live.

How much does it cost?

Mayotti requires a subscription, with monthly and yearly plans. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Your expenses and budget stay on your device. When you use AI features, the message, photo or recording is processed to give you an answer and isn't stored by us. See the privacy policy for details.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID, and you can restore it on a new phone.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Mayotti never asks for bank logins. You log spending by chat, voice or receipt photo, so you stay in control of what's recorded.

How do I get help?

Email support@mayotti.com or visit our support page.
