Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version

  • Your expenses and budget are stored only on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • When you use the AI features, the text, receipt photo or voice recording you send is processed by OpenAI to give you an answer. We don't keep it.
  • We never sell your data.

This policy explains what information the Mayotti iPhone app ("Mayotti", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at support@mayotti.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Mayotti doesn't have user accounts. The expenses you log, your budget, and your answers to the onboarding questions are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. Chat conversations are kept only while the app is open. We don't upload this data to our servers and can't see it.

Deleting the app deletes this data. If your iPhone backs up app data to iCloud or your computer, that backup is controlled by Apple and you, not by us.

2. Information processed when you use AI features

Mayotti's chat, receipt scanning and voice input use artificial intelligence provided by OpenAI. When you use one of these features:

  • Chat: your message and recent messages from the conversation are sent so the AI can understand context.
  • Receipt scanning: the photo you take or choose is sent so the AI can read it.
  • Voice input: your recording is sent so it can be turned into text.

This content passes through our server functions (hosted by Supabase) to OpenAI and the answer comes back to your phone. We don't store the content of your messages, photos or recordings. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as time of request and IP address) for a short period for security and reliability.

Under OpenAI's API policies, content sent through its API is not used to train its models, and may be retained for a limited period for abuse and misuse monitoring before being deleted.

Please don't include sensitive personal information (like full card numbers or passwords) in chats, photos or recordings.

3. Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat assigns your installation a random, anonymous ID and receives your App Store purchase information (such as which plan you bought, when, and its price) and basic device information (such as device model, iOS version, app version and country or region).

4. AI usage limits

To keep the service fair and affordable, subscriptions include a monthly number of AI requests. Our server keeps a count of AI requests per month for each anonymous RevenueCat ID. This count is not linked to your name or any other personal details.

5. Permissions on your iPhone

PermissionWhy we ask
CameraTo take a photo of a receipt, only when you choose to.

You can change these at any time in your iPhone's Settings. The rest of the app works without them. If you choose an existing photo instead, iOS shares only that photo with Mayotti, not your library.

6. How we use information

We use the information described above only to provide Mayotti's features, check your subscription, enforce monthly AI limits, keep the service secure and working, and respond if you contact us. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell or share it for advertising.

8. How long we keep information

  • Data on your device: until you delete it or the app.
  • AI request content: not stored by us; handled by OpenAI as described in section 2.
  • AI usage counts: kept while they're needed for monthly limits and then deleted.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat and Apple as needed to manage your purchases and meet legal obligations.

9. Your choices and rights

You can delete individual expenses in the app's History tab, or delete everything by deleting the app. You can cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don't have accounts and keep very little, there's usually nothing on our side to access. To make a request, email support@mayotti.com.

10. Children

Mayotti isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Security

Information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our server keys are kept on the server and never shipped in the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the little information we handle.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: support@mayotti.com
