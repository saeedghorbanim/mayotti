Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Mayotti → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I got a new phone or reinstalled. How do I get my subscription back?

Open Mayotti and tap Restore Purchases (or Log in on the welcome screen) while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Where are my expenses stored?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so expenses don't move to a new phone and are deleted if you delete the app. See our Privacy Policy.

Why did the AI say I've reached my limit?

Subscriptions include a monthly number of AI messages, receipt scans and voice notes. The count resets at the start of each month. You can still log expenses manually.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
